About UCIMS

UCIMS is a centralized digital registry developed for the healthcare services of the University of Southeastern Philippines.

It brings patient registration, consultation documentation, medical history, and certificate processing into one platform.

Our purpose

Support quality university healthcare by streamlining clinic workflows, maintaining accurate records, and providing access based on each user’s role.
