System Objective
The general objective of the proposed University Clinic Information Management System (UCIMS) is to develop a web-based, role-based platform that streamlines and automates the operational workflow of the university clinic, enhancing the efficiency of patient registration, consultation management, and clinical documentation while ensuring data integrity and user-specific access control.
What We Offer

Services Offered

The USeP University Clinic provides comprehensive health services to students and personnel.

Check-Up
General medical consultations and physical examinations conducted by licensed clinic physicians and nurses for students and staff.
Medicine Request
Patients may request available medicines from the clinic's pharmacy for treatment of minor illnesses and health conditions.
Follow-Up Consultation
Scheduled follow-up visits to monitor ongoing health conditions and review treatment progress after an initial consultation.
Medical Certificate Request
Issuance of official medical certificates for school, employment, OJT clearance, sick leave, and return-to-work or school purposes.
Background

About the System

The University Clinic Information Management System (UCIMS) is a web-based information system developed to serve as a centralized digital registry for the healthcare services of the University of Southeastern Philippines.

The project aims to modernize the management of clinic operations by integrating patient registration, consultation documentation, medical history tracking, and certificate processing into a unified digital platform. By replacing traditional paper-based documentation with a secure and responsive system, UCIMS provides an efficient solution for organizing clinical data and improving the accessibility of health records.

Ultimately, UCIMS supports the delivery of quality healthcare services within the university — ensuring that every student and personnel receives timely, well-documented, and properly managed clinical care.
